About Us

A Legacy of Public Excellence

Building the foundation of tomorrow's Nepal, one student at a time.

70+ Years of Quality Education

Founded in 2019 B.S. by Mr. Tirtha Prasad Dhakal, Kavresthali Secondary School is a government public school located in Tarakeshwor Municipality-2, Kavresthali, Kathmandu. Affiliated with the Nepal Government, we offer education from ECD through Grade 12.

Under Headmaster Prahlad Pokhrel and Director Mr. Shukdev Dhakal, our school continues to shape the next generation with dedication and integrity.
